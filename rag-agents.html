RAG & agents: diagnose the layer that failed

End-to-end accuracy hides where the system broke. Good AI engineering separates retrieval, ranking, generation, attribution, and action reliability.

RAG metrics

MetricQuestion
Recall@kDid relevant evidence appear in the top k?
Precision@kHow much of top k was actually useful?
MRRHow high was the first relevant result?
NDCGHow good was the ranked ordering when relevance has levels?
Pareto cut: evaluate retrieval separately from answer generation. Otherwise a “hallucination” may actually be a retrieval miss.

Query→Retrieve→Rerank→Generate→Cite

Agent reliability compounds

If each of 10 independent steps succeeds with 95% probability:

0.95¹⁰ ≈ 0.60

A “95% reliable” primitive can produce a much less reliable long workflow.

Pareto cut: shorten trajectories, checkpoint, verify, retry safely, and prefer deterministic tools where possible.

Expected value beats raw accuracy

If a correct automation saves ₹50 but a wrong one costs ₹10,000, then 99% accuracy can still have negative expected value.

EV = Σ probability(outcome) × value(outcome)
Pareto cut: the right threshold is often the one that maximizes expected utility under risk constraints—not the one that maximizes F1.
