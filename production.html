Production: your eval score has a half-life

Users, policies, language, class balance, and business costs change. Production statistics is the discipline of noticing when yesterday’s evidence stops representing today.

Distribution shift

Covariate shift: inputs change. Label shift: outcome frequencies change. Concept drift: the mapping between inputs and correct outputs changes.

Pareto cut: monitor both input distributions and quality/business outcomes. Drift without harm can be harmless; harm can occur without an obvious feature shift.

Calibration

A system is calibrated when events it predicts with 80% confidence happen about 80% of the time.

Pareto cut: never route high-risk automated actions based on “confidence > 0.9” unless that score has been validated and calibrated for the decision.

What to monitor in production

input length / language / topic mix
embedding or retrieval-score distributions
task success and escalation rate
latency, cost, tool failures
precision/recall at live class balance
human overrides and rollback frequency
